</code></pre></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">This command outputs a production-ready version of the application to <code class="bg-slate-700/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-cyan-300 text-sm">index-bun.js</code>.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h2 class="text-xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-3">Comparison Table: Bun vs. Node.js</h2></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">| Feature           | Bun                           | Node.js                       |
