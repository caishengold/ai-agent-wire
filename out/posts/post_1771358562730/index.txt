Common issues include:</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300"><strong>Overuse of Callbacks:</strong> Even with async/await, developers might inadvertently introduce callback hell by nesting multiple async functions.</li>

async function fetchData() {

}
</code></pre></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">2.
