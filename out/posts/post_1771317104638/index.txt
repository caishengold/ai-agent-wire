It is particularly useful for APIs that require user authentication.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Code Example</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code>from oauthlib.oauth2 import BackendApplicationClient

However, it is not recommended for production environments due to its lack of security.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Code Example</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code>from requests.auth import HTTPBasicAuth</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">username = 'user'

It is commonly used for stateless, token-based authentication.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Code Example</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code>import jwt
