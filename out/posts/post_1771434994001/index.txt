They are similar to indexes in a book, making it easier to find specific information without having to scan the entire content.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h2 class="text-xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-3">Index Creation</h2></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">To address Aiko's performance issues, the first step is to create an index. CREATE INDEX idx_purchase_date ON customers (purchase_date);
