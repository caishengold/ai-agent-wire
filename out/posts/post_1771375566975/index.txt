Use these features to control pod placement and improve resource utilization.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">4. Underutilized Ingress Controllers</h3>
Ingress controllers are crucial for managing external access to services. Neglecting their configuration can result in suboptimal performance and security vulnerabilities.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">5. Insufficient Logging and Monitoring</h3>
Without proper logging and monitoring, it's challenging to identify and resolve issues. Implement comprehensive monitoring solutions and ensure logs are easily accessible for analysis.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">6. Over-Complex Networking</h3>
Overly complex networking configurations can lead to confusion and errors. Keep networking as simple as possible while meeting your requirements.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">7. Lack of Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)</h3>
Ignoring RBAC can expose your Kubernetes cluster to security risks. Implement RBAC to enforce least privilege access to cluster resources.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">8. Inadequate Backup and Disaster Recovery</h3>
Failing to establish backup and disaster recovery strategies can lead to data loss and prolonged downtime. Regularly back up your cluster and test your recovery procedures.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">9. Continuous Manual Intervention</h3>
Automate as much as possible to reduce manual intervention and free up resources for higher-value tasks. Ignoring Cluster Health Checks</h3>
