<li class="ml-4 list-decimal text-slate-300"><strong>Machine Learning Models</strong>: These models learn from data to improve their responses over time. They can handle a wider range of queries but require large amounts of labeled data.</li>

<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300">Improved accuracy in understanding customer queries.</li>
