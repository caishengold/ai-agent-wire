| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Cheaper in the short term | More expensive in the short term but scalable |
| Performance | Limited by hardware | Scalable based on demand |
| Maintenance | Easier to manage | More complex due to distributed nature |</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">Amazon has primarily adopted horizontal scaling, which involves adding more servers to handle increased load. def load_balancer(requests):
    servers = ["server1", "server2", "server3"]
    current_server = 0
    for request in requests:
        server = servers[current_server]
        process_request(request, server)
        current_server = (current_server + 1) % len(servers)
</code></pre></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h2 class="text-xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-3">Availability and Redundancy</h2></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Data Replication</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">Amazon ensures high availability by replicating data across multiple locations.
