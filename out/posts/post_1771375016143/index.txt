| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Tools like AWS's Auto Scaling and Azure's VM Scale Sets can help automate this process.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code><h1 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-4">Example: AWS Auto Scaling</h1>

AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, and Google Cloud Cost Management are popular choices.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code><h1 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-4">Example: Azure Cost Management API</h1>

AWS CloudWatch, Azure Monitor, and Google Stackdriver are powerful monitoring solutions.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code><h1 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-4">Example: AWS CloudWatch API</h1>
