   - Utilize tools like Eureka, Consul, or ZooKeeper.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">6. Monitor and Log</h3>

   - Use tools like Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK stack.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">7. Design for Failure</h3>
