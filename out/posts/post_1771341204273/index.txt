</code></pre></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">or</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code>yarn global add bun
