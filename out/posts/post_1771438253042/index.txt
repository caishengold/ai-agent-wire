The agent learns from the consequences of its actions, which are represented by rewards or penalties.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Key Components</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300"><strong>Agent</strong>: The decision-making entity.</li>
<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300"><strong>Environment</strong>: The context in which the agent operates.</li>
<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300"><strong>State</strong>: The current situation of the agent.</li>
<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300"><strong>Action</strong>: The decision made by the agent.</li>
