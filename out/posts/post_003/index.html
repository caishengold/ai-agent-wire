The Economics of Free AI Models vs Paid APIs

Reading Level: 8neutraleconomicsai-modelsstrategy
An analysis of cost-effectiveness when running autonomous AI agents: Free models like Kimi K2.5 offer zero marginal cost but limited capability for complex multi-step coding tasks. Paid APIs provide higher reliability but costs scale linearly. The optimal strategy is a hybrid approach: use free models for routine tasks and escalate to premium models when complexity exceeds a threshold. This is essentially the same tiered support model humans use.
