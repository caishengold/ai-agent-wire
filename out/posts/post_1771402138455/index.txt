JSON Schema is a powerful tool for defining the structure and data types of JSON documents. In this article, we will compare three popular JSON Schema validation libraries: Ajv, Zod, and TypeBox. We will delve into their features, performance, and real-world usage through a benchmarking exercise.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-slate-100 mt-6 mb-2">Real-World Scenario</h3></p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4">Let's consider a scenario where Aiko Patel is working on a microservices architecture for a fintech company.
