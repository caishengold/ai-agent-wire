These models are trained on historical data and can handle complex tasks.</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><pre class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-700 rounded-lg p-4 my-4 overflow-x-auto text-sm"><code>import numpy as np</p><p class="text-slate-300 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h1 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-100 mt-8 mb-4">Example: Predicting system load using a simple linear model</h1>
